# Your study account

On a site with password sign-in enabled, enter your username and password to open
your study desk. You can sign in on another computer and continue the same hosted
study history: saved responses, drafts and flashcard reviews stay with your account.
The sidebar shows who is signed in and includes **Sign out**, including on mobile.

If **Create account** is available, choose a username and a password, then confirm
the password. Usernames are case-insensitive, 3–32 characters long, start with a
letter or number and may also contain dots, underscores and hyphens. Passwords
must be 10–200 characters and cannot consist only of spaces. If a setup-code field
appears, use the code supplied by the person operating the site.

The first account on this site keeps the study progress already saved here. Later
hosted accounts start with their own empty history. Registration normally closes
after the first account; the site operator can choose to reopen it.

Keep the password in your password manager. This version has no password-reset
flow. If sign-in fails, check the username and password. After repeated failures,
wait 15 minutes before trying again. Sessions last up to 30 days; if a session
expires, the sign-in screen returns. Sign out when finished on a shared computer.

Optional AI feedback remains a separate choice for each request, and its daily
budget applies to your hosted account. Signing in does not make model calls or
turn practice into independent evidence.

The default local app needs no account. When optional local password mode is
enabled, all its accounts share the same local study history. Local login does
not synchronize history to another computer.
